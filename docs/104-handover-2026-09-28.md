**Finding (2026-09-28): our SA3 checkpoints are the wrong ones for generation.** All three local SA3 models are the
`-base` (pre-trained) versions: `models/stable-audio-3-{small-sfx,small-music,medium}-base`. Stability's own model
card for `stabilityai/stable-audio-3-small-sfx-base` says: "This is the base (pre-trained) model intended for
fine-tuning. If you are looking to generate audio directly, please use Stable Audio 3 Small SFX instead." The release
models add adversarial post-training (fewer steps, better fidelity). For the base models the card specifies exactly
what we already run (`steps=50, cfg_scale=7.0, sampler_type="euler"`), so our port and sampler are correct for base;
the quality gap is the checkpoint.

  `Stable-Audio-3-{Small-SFX,Small-Music,Medium}-GGUF/*-{f16,q8_0}.gguf`. Small SFX f16 is downloaded (above).
- audio.cpp's defaults for these: **sampler `pingpong`, 8 steps, CFG 1.0** (CFG 2.0 for a "guided" music case;
  `examples/audio.cpp/tests/stable_audio/stable_audio_warm_bench_cases.json`). Pingpong step
  (`examples/audio.cpp/src/models/stable_audio/rf_dit.cpp` ~676-705):
  `denoised = x - sigma*v; x = (1 - sigma_next)*denoised + sigma_next*fresh_noise`.
