### E. Product surface

- [x] **D2** *(resolved 2026-11-01, see below)* (new, from D1) Restart-proof test reliability: `HotSessionGreedyReplayTests.ColdSession_RealModel_CrossProcessRestore_MatchesFullGreedyReplay` timed out at three minutes when run through its xunit parent. **Root cause (found with `dotnet-stack`, installed with the owner's approval):** a deadlock by design, not a flaky exit. Every heavy test class holds the machine-wide mutex `Global\OpenTailStingray.HeavyTests` for its lifetime (`HeavyTestBase`); this test starts a child process of the same test executable, whose `HeavyTestBase` constructor waits for the same mutex (up to two hours) while the parent waits for the child, so the child never reached its test body. The test cannot have passed since the gate was added. **Fix:** the parent sets `OPENTAIL_HEAVY_GATE_HELD_BY_PARENT=1` for its child and the Sessions `HeavyTestBase` then skips acquiring the gate (it still honours `STINGRAY_RUN_HEAVY_TESTS`). The test now passes in 5.3 s with real SmolLM2-1.7B weights (a persist child and a restore child in separate processes, restored session equal to a full greedy replay), and reverting the one line brings the three-minute timeout back. **Correction of my earlier note here:** it said the persist child had finished its work and then failed to exit; that was wrong. The manifest and packs I saw were leftovers of earlier failed runs (their folders were not deleted), and the child had not started its test body. The four `opentail_restart_proof_*` folders in the temp directory are those leftovers.
