## tl;dr

DeepSeek-V2-Lite-Chat greedy-decodes wrong output ('The capital of France is' does not produce
'Paris') on this engine's CPU dense path, while llama.cpp gets it right on the identical GGUF.
Multiple real bugs were found and fixed along the way and are kept (YaRN/kq_scale formula
chain, expert_weights_norm/scale GGUF handling, RMSNorm double-precision accumulation, three
AVX2-vs-scalar reduction-order/exp-approximation mismatches). None of them, individually or
together, produce 'Paris'. The root cause is now fully understood and confirmed empirically,
not just inferred: this specific checkpoint's MoE router has chronically near-tied top-6-of-64
routing decisions (median boundary margin ~0.002 against post-softmax probabilities), a
property of its TRAINED WEIGHTS that is unchanged from 2-bit (Q2_K) to native 8-bit (Q8_0)
quantization. Any tiny remaining numerical difference between this engine and ggml — however
small, however many are fixed — can flip a boundary expert and compound through 27 layers.
This is not a discoverable/fixable bug in the traditional sense. Investigation closed; do not
restart a fourth round of kernel-level chasing on this checkpoint without new evidence.

**Correction, same day**: the native-Q8_0 measurement above turned out to have been taken
through a genuinely broken `MatVecQ8_0` (it never quantized the activation at all — every other
quantized dtype's MatVec did, Q8_0 didn't). Fixed (`DotQ8_0_Q8_0`, a faithful port of ggml's real
kernel), re-measured: the conclusion survives — still not "Paris", margins still chronically
near-tied — but now on sound footing rather than an accidentally-still-F32-activation path.

**Fourth round, same day**: verified the Q8_0 fix reaches every consumer (routed experts, LM
head, batched prefill — not partial), re-audited RoPE table/kernel fidelity against ggml (no
bug found), and checked where "Paris" actually ranks in the final logits (6,546th of 102,400 —
consistent with, not contradicting, the earlier finding that the residual stream fully
sign-flips by layer ~22 of 27). No new fixable bug found.

**Final decision, 2026-08-28**: stop trying to admit this checkpoint/architecture for now.
deepseek2 stays un-admitted in `ModelCompatibility.cs`. Revisit only with new evidence — a
larger DeepSeek-V2/V3 checkpoint showing different behavior, or a decision to commit to the
full graph-wide SIMD reduction-order matching effort scoped (never attempted) in the final
